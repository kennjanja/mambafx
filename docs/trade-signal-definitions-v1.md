# Trade Signal mechanical definitions v1.0.1

**Status: FROZEN v1.0.1.** The user approved STOP 0 and the Model A swept-level, confirmation and attribution amendment. The new hash and previous v1.0 hash are recorded in the validation log. A later change requires a new version, hash and explicit approval. Chart-pattern geometry and evidence-family normalization are deferred to a v1.1 addendum for Batch 2; this version does not authorize freezing those Batch 2 comparisons. These definitions are audit criteria, not claims that production conforms.

Terminology: `DECIDED` means expressly directed by the user; `ACCEPT` means the current rule is accepted for this audit, not independently proven correct; `VALIDATE-LATER` means retain the value, not optimize it; `PROPOSED` means no approval yet; `TEST` means verification is required; `CHANGE` denotes a source-level conflict with a decided requirement, not an independently reproduced defect; `SUSPECTED DEFECT` means a concrete reproducer has not established it. The prior [source audit](trade-signal-definitions-audit.md) records current behavior, but the decided rules below take precedence where they differ.

## 1. Data, time and availability

Input is an ordered series of OHLC candles `(epoch, open, high, low, close)` for one symbol and timeframe `g` seconds, a finite decision time `T`, and approved schedule/completeness metadata. Epoch denotes candle open. A candle is available iff `epoch + g <= T`; all decisions, validations and state transitions are evaluated after close. `eventIndex`/event time identify the candle where the event occurred; `confirmationIndex` and confirmation time identify the first closed candle establishing it. Decision time may be later. Do not backdate the latter to the former.

Valid candles have finite epoch/OHLC, `high >= max(open,close)`, `low <= min(open,close)`, `high >= low`, and strictly increasing epochs. On approved continuous markets a missing bar, duplicate epoch or dropped invalid candle is a data fault. Report it; no structure, pool, line, range or leg may span it. Analyze only the contiguous tail after the most recent fault; fewer than 60 post-fault candles for a needed timeframe blocks the signal. No OHLC is synthesized or interpolated. The approved inventory is the ten `R_10`, `R_25`, `R_50`, `R_75`, `R_100`, `1HZ10V`, `1HZ25V`, `1HZ50V`, `1HZ75V`, `1HZ100V` volatility indices, continuous 24/7; Step and Jump are excluded. Existing `sanitize` does not enforce this policy.

ATR is 14-period Wilder ATR: `TR[0]=high[0]-low[0]`; for `i>0`, `TR[i]=max(high[i]-low[i], abs(high[i]-close[i-1]), abs(low[i]-close[i-1]))`. `ATR[13]` is the mean of `TR[0..13]`; `ATR[i]=(13*ATR[i-1]+TR[i])/14` thereafter. Before index 13 ATR is unavailable. References to preceding ATR mean `ATR[i-1]`, known before candle `i`. Never substitute or backfill future values. An ATR-dependent observation is unavailable and cannot qualify or validate; a model requiring it cannot qualify, but unrelated models are not blocked merely by that missing ATR.

For all outputs, append-only future candles must not alter complete decision-relevant as-of state. Full comparison includes IDs, levels, confirmation times, state, invalidation, parentage, locations, ranking, targets and final decision. Retrospective sweep reaction is descriptive only after its complete observation window and must never affect signal decisions.

## 2. Pivots, trend and structure

For order `n`, high pivot `p` requires `high[p] > high[p-k]` for each `1<=k<=n`, and `high[p] >= high[p+k]` for each `1<=k<=n`; low pivots reverse inequalities. Confirmation index `p+n`, confirmation timestamp candle close; all required candles must be available. Existing default permits right-side equality but not left-side equality; strict helper mode rejects right ties too. Internal order 3; swing order 10; levels/trendlines order 5; inducement minor order 2; general chart patterns order 3. Provisional tail pivots cannot become confirmed evidence.

**DECIDED:** first close strictly beyond the latest eligible confirmed high or low is `initial-break`, not BOS; it sets trend direction for classifying later breaks, supplies no BOS or CHoCH evidence and cannot satisfy a model's BOS requirement. Thereafter a close strictly beyond an available unconsumed pivot in current trend direction is BOS; opposite direction is CHoCH and updates trend. Each broken pivot is consumed. MSS is a CHoCH flagged when same-direction displacement ended on that candle or either of its two preceding candles. Displacement and BOS/CHoCH may share a candle. The optional production wick-break mode is not the Trade Signal default. `structure.js:13-47` labels the first break BOS: **CHANGE**.

Displacement: directional non-doji body `abs(close-open) >= 1.5*ATR[i-1]`; or three consecutive candles of the same nonzero direction with `abs(close[i]-open[i-2]) >= 2.25*ATR[i-3]`. Confirmation is end candle `i`; suppress a multi-candle event ending at a single-candle displacement. These numeric thresholds are current defaults, **ACCEPT / VALIDATE-LATER**, not calibrated results.

## 3. Liquidity and inducement

Each confirmed internal order-3 high forms buy-side candidate liquidity; each low forms sell-side candidate liquidity. **DECIDED:** assign a new pivot to the nearest active same-side non-pending pivot cluster whose distance from current cluster level is `<=0.25*ATR[confirmation]`; break exact distance ties by earliest stable pool ID. Otherwise create a new pool with stable identity. Level is maximum constituent high for buy side or minimum constituent low for sell side. A single pivot is a candidate; at least two qualifying touches or a valid complete adjacent period level are significant. `0.25 ATR` is **ACCEPT / VALIDATE-LATER**. Existing first-match cluster selection is **CHANGE**.

Day starts UTC midnight, week starts Monday 00:00 UTC. Previous-day/week high/low use the immediately adjacent completed period only; every expected candle at the granularity must exist, including across provider outages. Partial/incomplete/missing periods yield no valid period pool and are reported. Do not substitute older buckets or add named session pools for these synthetic indices. Keep reference opens distinct. Existing code flags only an initial partial bucket and does not check adjacent bucket IDs: **CHANGE / SUSPECTED DEFECT**.

**DECIDED strict sweep:** buy-side candidate iff `high > level && close < level`; sell-side iff `low < level && close > level`. Confirm only when the immediate next closed candle also has `close < level` for buy, `close > level` for sell; equality fails. Preserve pool ID, candidate ID, swept level/extreme, candidate and confirmation candle and timestamps; mark the pool swept only on confirmation. Model A requires confirmation, never a candidate alone. This strict reclaim applies to liquidity, inducement, spring and upthrust. Existing liquidity and inducement permit equality; Model A reads candidates: **CHANGE**.

Candidate has exactly one confirmation opportunity, the next closed candle. Strictly reclaimed confirms; strictly wrong-side closes produce `sweep-failed`, stamped on that candle, retaining candidate history, and invalidate the pool. Equality lapses the candidate, leaves the pool active and eligible, and records no confirmed sweep. Active nonpending wick-through with close outside invalidates immediately without a failed-sweep event. Expired non-pending pivot pools are ineligible after age `>300`; period pools expire at period change, but pending pools retain existing expiry deferral.

Inducement uses an internal BOS and same-direction displacement ending on BOS or either prior two candles, with an opposite-side protected order-10 pivot confirmed by impulse start; then a later order-2 minor pullback pivot, not an already confirmed major pivot nor within `0.1*ATR` at minor confirmation of another already confirmed major same-side pivot, and no opposing CHoCH since BOS through pivot confirmation. Within 20 bars after minor confirmation, a strictly reclaimed wick sweep must occur; within the next three bars, a same-direction displacement starting strictly after the sweep must finish. The 20 and 3 limits are inclusive. Confirm at displacement end.

Existing invalidation precedence in the forward loop: (1) wick takes protected pivot, (2) opposing internal CHoCH confirmed, (3) while formed, age `>20`, (4) while formed, close through minor pivot before sweep, (5) shared classifier marks significant protected structural liquidity, (6) while swept, age since sweep `>3`; then qualifying displacement can confirm. The detector stops its loop once confirmed. **DECIDED:** after confirmation, Model F rechecks protected level taken, opposing CHoCH and wick beyond sweep extreme at decision time; formation limits apply only until confirmation, then the Model F sequence age governs.

## 4. Zones, mitigations and edges

**DECIDED:** candle/zone intersection iff `high >= bottom && low <= top`. A gap entirely past a zone is not a touch; a first interaction from the far side is not a normal-direction retest. On a candle that both intersects and closes beyond the far side, invalidation takes precedence: it cannot be a valid retest or mitigation. Closed candle only. Each zone, candidate, promotion, retest, invalidation and parent retains a stable identity.

Order block: last opposite-colour candle within ten bars before displacement start; full candle `[low,high]` is zone; body bounds and `(low+high)/2` are metadata. Candidate promoted by same-direction internal BOS/CHoCH on displacement end through five bars thereafter. Close strictly beyond far side invalidates; otherwise candidate expires after the window; confirmed zone expires at age `>300`. Merge same-direction OBs at overlap `>=0.5` of narrower range; preserve original and merged identities and provenance. Existing production retains one object and merge count but not all parent IDs: **CHANGE**.

**DECIDED proximal edges:** bullish OB, demand and bullish breaker use `top`; bearish OB, supply and bearish breaker use `bottom`. Failed bearish OB creates bullish breaker; failed bullish creates bearish breaker. Retest uses current direction and correct approach side, not parent direction or penetration depth. Approach is from above iff the closed candle immediately before first intersection closed strictly above `top`; from below iff it closed strictly below `bottom`. Gaps past the zone are not touches. Record deepest penetration independently. Merged zones retain their merged ID and list of parent IDs. Existing model events use bottom for both directions: **CHANGE**.

Breaker: failed OB is a potential opposite-direction breaker with same bounds and original parent identity, but becomes eligible only upon same-direction CHoCH confirmed on or after the failure candle and within Model D sequence age. Retests before eligibility do not count. OB/breaker age 300, FVG/IFVG 200, supply/demand 300 bars. Current engine immediately creates active breaker on failure: **CHANGE**.

FVG at third candle `j`: bullish iff `low[j]>high[j-2]`, bearish iff `high[j]<low[j-2]`; gap `>=0.4*ATR[j-1]`. Bull bounds `[high[j-2], low[j]]`, bear `[high[j], low[j-2]]`; formation index `j-1`, confirmation index `j`. Preserve first/middle/third candle IDs, ATR and size. Formation-quality requirements: gap/third range `>=0.5`, first relevant wick/first range `<=0.35`, third relevant wick/third range `<=0.35`, and body difference divided by larger body `<=0.2`, with a nonzero larger body. First correction intersects on bars `j+1..j+3` inclusive; penetration greater than 50% is deep. These numbers and filters are **ACCEPT / VALIDATE-LATER**.

Wick fill is deepest penetration through the zone. **DECIDED:** a fully wick-filled FVG cannot qualify Model C; it may subsequently invert on a strict far-side close and qualify Model I. IFVG inherits parent bounds and opposite direction. Its first closed intersecting candle from the valid approach side that does not invalidate consumes the first-retest opportunity even without a trade entry. Existing `eligibleFvgs` does not exclude fully filled parents from C: **CHANGE / SUSPECTED DEFECT**.

Other accepted present definitions: supply/demand is full high-low range of one to three base candles immediately preceding displacement, each range `<=0.6*ATR` at that candle; confirmation at displacement end; expire age `>300`, far-side close invalidates. S/R uses order-5 pivots within 0.25 ATR of the first matching current cluster, level midpoint of extrema, close-through buffered by `0.25*ATR[i-1]` flips role, age `>400` from last touch expires. Their causal reconstruction, merge identities and exact directional model eligibility need independent verification. Rejection block is a confirmed internal pivot wick `>=2*max(body,1e-12)` and `>=0.5*range`; BPR is positive overlap of opposite FVGs confirmed within ten bars. No distinct mitigation-block detector.

## 5. Ranges, locations and lines

**DECIDED dealing ranges:** bias timeframe uses confirmed order-10 pivots; entry timeframe uses confirmed order-5 pivots. Select most recent confirmed high and low independently, distinct with `H>L`; no alternation required. Preserve anchor IDs, indices, confirmations and timeframe. A newer qualifying pivot replaces the corresponding anchor. At midpoint `M=(H+L)/2`, long is eligible only for `L<=price<M`, short only for `M<price<=H`. Exact midpoint and outside `[L,H]` are ineligible for both. This directional qualification is separate from valid-zone proximity and model confirmation. Anchor choice is VALIDATE-LATER. Existing premium/discount uses order-5 latest high/low and exposes full bounds: **CHANGE**.

**DECIDED trendlines are display-only:** immutable identity is the two same-type order-5 confirmed pivot IDs, at least five candle indices apart. Two anchors give Candidate; a third distinct confirmed same-type pivot within `0.25*ATR[q.index-1]` of line price at its pivot index gives Valid only if no disqualifying flag/invalidation; confirmation cannot precede third pivot confirmation. The line equation through `(i1,p1),(i2,p2)` is `p1+(p2-p1)*(i-i1)/(i2-i1)`. Label rising support, falling resistance or counter-trend; zero slope is flat with type from pivots.

Check all closed candles after anchor 1 through decision, including second-anchor confirmation interval. A wick beyond a support/resistance line by `<=0.1*ATR[i-1]` is tolerated. More than this, without close beyond, sets permanent violated-by-wick; a previously Valid line becomes Flagged and cannot be Valid. A close strictly beyond the line with zero buffer invalidates at that close. Missing ATR makes that candle unavailable for touch or tolerance checks. No automatic supersession: each pair remains its own line until close-through breaks it. Existing code uses two touches, 0.25 prior-ATR close buffer and different touch ATR: **CHANGE**.

## 6. Wyckoff-style and candle reactions

General spring/upthrust: last 20 closed bars define `L=min(low)`, `H=max(high)`; spring iff current `low<L && close>L`, upthrust iff `high>H && close<H`; timestamp is current close. Pattern SOS/SOW: close `>H`/`<L` and body `>=1.5*ATR[i-1]`. These are price-only descriptions, not evidence of genuine volume, accumulation, distribution, or actual absorption. Production's range-as-effort label is a price proxy, not volume.

Model J range: high/low of 20 bars preceding breakout, width `<=8*ATR[i-1]`; established at close of last window candle. Require at least two upper and two lower tests inside this window, including bars defining extremes. Upper test: open in `[L,H]`, intersect range, `high>=H-0.25*ATR[i-1]`, `close<=H`; lower: open in range, intersect, `low<=L+0.25*ATR[i-1]`, `close>=L`. Same-side tests separated by at least three bars; a candle counts at most one side, whichever boundary is closer to its close, with exact tie counting neither. Window 20-close efficiency ratio `abs(last-first)/sum(abs(close[k]-close[k-1])) <=0.3`; zero denominator is zero. Thresholds 0.25, 3 and 0.3 are VALIDATE-LATER. Existing breakout requires strict close beyond high/low and absolute body `>=1.5*ATR[i-1]`. Within 30 subsequent bars inclusive, LPS requires low `<=H+0.25*ATR[i-1]` and close `>H`; LPSY requires high `>=L-0.25*ATR[i-1]` and close `<L`; close through opposite range boundary invalidates. Range, breakout and retest share ID. Call them range breakout/retest (Wyckoff-style); required changes for boundary count/ranging: **CHANGE**.

Directional reactions: pin bar body/range `<=0.35`, candle range `>=0.5*ATR[i-1]` if available, dominant wick `>=2*body` and `>2*opposite wick`; lower wick is bullish, upper bearish. Engulfing: prior/current nonzero opposite-colour bodies, current body envelops prior body inclusively and is strictly larger. Three-bar bullish reversal: first candle bearish, middle low below first low, third bullish close above first high; bearish mirror. Reactions are confirmed at last candle close. Neutral inside, outside and NR4/NR7 are not directional reactions. **DECIDED:** reaction must interact with the same model zone/level, be in the same scenario and direction, and be no older than two closed bars at decision; location identity, retest/reaction relationship and valid same-bar intersection are mandatory.

## 7. Model identity chains and ordering

All models require a directional bias, separately qualified location and ordered entry confirmation. **DECIDED:** every chain uses explicit identities and causally related objects, not mere chronological proximity; generic matcher use is **CHANGE**. Confirmation cannot precede the close that establishes each prerequisite. Displacement with BOS/CHoCH on one candle is allowed where otherwise valid; a retest and reaction can share a candle only if it intersects the same zone or level and independently meets both definitions. Any other same-bar ordering cannot bypass an explicit later-close confirmation.

| Model | Required identity chain and qualification |
|---|---|
| A | Significant pool or admitted spring/upthrust -> strict candidate and confirmed sweep ID/extreme -> uniquely assigned directional displacement -> related CHoCH/MSS, using the amendment below. Matched sweep location; closed wick beyond the sweep extreme invalidates. Candidate alone cannot qualify. |
| B | Displacement -> related BOS -> parent OB creation/promotion -> first eligible same-OB proximal retest -> reaction at that OB. Strict close beyond OB far side invalidates. |
| C | Displacement -> same FVG's creation, qualification, correction and retest -> reaction at that same FVG. Fully wick-filled parent ineligible for continuation. Strict close beyond its far side invalidates. |
| D | Parent OB -> failure -> required opposite CHoCH -> same-parent breaker -> first eligible same-breaker retest -> reaction at that breaker. Strict close beyond breaker far side invalidates. |
| F | Directional BOS/protected major pivot -> inducement identity -> confirmed minor pivot -> strict minor sweep -> later directional displacement, each same scenario; recheck all applicable invalidations at actual decision. Closed-candle wick beyond sweep extreme invalidates. |
| G | BOS ID and broken structural level -> related displacement -> retest of that exact level -> reaction to same retest. Strict close beyond broken level invalidates. |
| I | Parent FVG -> quality and correction -> inversion -> child IFVG ID -> first qualifying inverse retest. Parent may have been fully wick-filled; no separate reaction required. |
| J | Established range ID with both boundary tests and ranging condition -> same-range SOS/SOW -> same-range LPS/LPSY. No separate reaction required. |

Bind BOS/CHoCH to a same-direction displacement ending on its break candle or either of the preceding two bars. Bind OB to the displacement that generated it and retest/reaction to the same zone or level ID. Multiple bound candidates form separately ranked scenarios, except Model A's unique assignment below. Reaction is required only for B, C, D and G.

The approved provisional sequence ages for A/B/C/D/F/G/I/J are `30/30/12/40/20/30/12/50` closed entry bars for every profile, measured inclusively from the first required event's confirmation through the decision candle. Beyond the limit the scenario is Expired. These are VALIDATE-LATER placeholders, not tuned values.

Zone invalidation applies from zone confirmation; scenario invalidation from its first confirmed event through decision, with invalidation beating touch, retest and reaction on a shared candle. For B/C/D/I bullish close `<bottom`, bearish close `>top`; G bullish close `<broken BOS level`, bearish `>level`; J bullish close `<range low`, bearish `>range high`. These mappings are VALIDATE-LATER; J's opposite boundary is deliberately loose and must be re-examined during validation.

### Model A sweep-to-displacement binding amendment

Pool sweep `sweptLevelId` is its pool ID. A Model A spring/upthrust uses `SPR|20|windowStartIndex|candleIndex` or `UPT|20|windowStartIndex|candleIndex`, based only on data available at confirmation. Production does not provide this identity: **CHANGE**. Preserve spring/upthrust event ID, independently established pool and pool-sweep IDs, any structural-range ID, displacement ID and CHoCH/MSS ID without fabricating them.

The general pattern-engine spring/upthrust display event remains distinct. A Model A event exists only after its next closed candle strictly confirms reclaim of the prior 20-bar boundary: candidate spring `low<L && close>L`, confirmation close `>L`; candidate upthrust `high>H && close<H`, confirmation close `<H`. Equality does not qualify or confirm. Only the Model A-confirmed event receives its swept-level ID. An unconfirmed display event cannot bind.

Group sweeps by `(side, candidateIndex, sweep extreme)`; a pool sweep and spring/upthrust with the same candidate, confirmation index and extreme share one group. One group is one liquidity confirmation for matched-event counting. Group invalidation, retirement and age use its extreme and confirmation.

Pool attribution to a spring/upthrust requires that the candle setting its `L` or `H` is a constituent pivot of that pool and `pool.level` equals that boundary. If no pool or more than one pool passes, attribution is ambiguous: assign no pool ID, list unattributed candidates, retain the independent spring/upthrust event and count its group once. Do not select by order, ID or proximity.

For displacement `D`, a group is eligible exactly when confirmed, non-invalidated, non-retired, opposite side to `D`, candidate index `<=D.startIndex`, confirmation index `<=D.endIndex`, and no closed candle from candidate through `D.endIndex` has a wick beyond the group's extreme. Select latest confirmation index, then most extreme (highest for buy-side, lowest for sell-side), then earliest numeric candidate index. No pool-versus-spring preference; never use lexicographic numeric ordering. `D` binds one group. At `D.endIndex`, retire all eligible same-side groups across both types. CHoCH/MSS binds to `D` under the rule above. Closed-candle wick beyond the bound extreme invalidates, even if close reclaims; age runs from group confirmation through decision. Production's earliest generic event matching is **CHANGE**.

Required fixtures include passing exact attribution; missing member provenance; two passing pools; unequal pool level; spring without pool; different-candle same-extreme sweeps; later-confirming spring and upthrust; equal confirmation with different extremes; numeric candidate tie ordering (9 before 10); strict equality and missing next confirmation; unconfirmed display event; cross-type retirement; group-extreme invalidation; several eligible groups; one-group matched-event counting; and truncation invariance at every decision point. Also retain the earlier Model A fixtures: earlier sweep invalidated by wick, later eligible sweep, reuse after retirement, candidate candle equal displacement start, confirmation after displacement end, and opposite-side sweep.

Per family, strongest bullish minus strongest bearish, normalized as in `src/analysis/tradeSignalArchitecture.js:13-26`; lean requires `abs(balance)>=0.35` and at least two agreeing net groups. HTF conflicts cap lean to inconclusive. These are **ACCEPT / VALIDATE-LATER**, not probabilities; complete normalization definition is deferred to v1.1.

**DECIDED:** an inconclusive entry structure does not qualify a continuation model; no fallback to last entry swing break when entry evidence is inconclusive. Production uses an entry-evidence lean when present and falls back to `swingBias` otherwise: **CHANGE**.

Location direction and validity, then complete model, must be independently established; a lifecycle label does not authorize an order. Existing lifecycle states include No Setup, Bias Identified, Watching, Approaching Location, Trigger Detected, Signal Active, Closed and terminal invalidated/expired/opposed/insufficient states. **DECIDED ranking:** eligible candidates sorted by required correctly identity-matched event count descending, then fixed deterministic priority `A,B,C,D,F,G,I,J`, then stable scenario ID. The priority is only a deterministic tie-break and implies nothing about quality. Do not use shared invalidation distance or substitute a generic swing invalidation.

**DECIDED target:** collect the existing eligible target set from both entry and bias timeframes, same symbol, available at decision, not invalid/already passed, strictly ahead of the latest closed entry candle's close. No related-leg requirement. Compare absolute distance; exact tie prefers entry timeframe, then earliest ID. Preserve target ID and timeframe.
