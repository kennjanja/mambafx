# Trade Signal Model A Batch 1 protocol

Status: preregistered protocol; no Batch 1 outcome has been computed or viewed. Freeze this file and record its SHA-256 before any outcome run. The final holdout remains locked until the conforming code commit, complete data manifest, and split boundaries are pinned.

## Question and eligible observations

Estimate whether confirmed Model A spring/upthrust setups in each attribution group outperform matched non-Model-A Trade Signal entries out of sample. Use the ten approved Deriv symbols (`R_10`, `R_25`, `R_50`, `R_75`, `R_100`, `1HZ10V`, `1HZ25V`, `1HZ50V`, `1HZ75V`, `1HZ100V`) and the three fixed profiles as configured in `src/analysis/tradeSignal.js`: Scalp (3-minute entry candles, 180-second hold), Intraday (5-minute entry candles, 1,800-second hold), and Swing (15-minute entry candles, 3,600-second hold). Collect and validate the six relevant candle timeframes (3m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h and 4h) for feature construction, entry and expiry. No profile, symbol, timeframe, or date may be selected based on outcomes.

An eligible observation is one unique, next-close-confirmed Model A group which is selected as the primary model in a `Signal Active` Trade Signal for a symbol/profile. The decision, direction, group ID, attribution fields, entry time and price must be recorded from the committed production path. Count a co-swept spring/upthrust and pool as one group. Do not count a group more than once for the same symbol/profile. Assign the study group solely by recorded `matchCount`: attributed = 1; unattributed = 0; ambiguous = 2 or more. Preserve the frozen attribution ambiguity fields without using `attributionAmbiguous` alone to relabel zero matches as multi-pool.

The matched baseline is one non-Model-A primary `Signal Active` Trade Signal entry with the same symbol, profile, direction and hold duration, selected from the same UTC 10-calendar-day block. Match without replacement to the nearest entry time; break exact time ties by stable event ID. A baseline entry carrying any Model A group is ineligible. Report unmatched Model A observations and unused controls; do not silently substitute another symbol, profile, direction, block, or outcome horizon.

## Outcome and estimand

Use the closed entry-timeframe candle at the actual signal decision as the entry price and the closed candle whose close is exactly `entry candle close time + profile holdSec` as the expiry price. A bullish entry wins only if expiry close is strictly greater than entry close; a bearish entry wins only if strictly lower. Equal prices are losses and separately reported ties. Do not use intrabar highs/lows, targets, stops, later ticks, forward-filled prices, or a stale expiry candle. Missing or ambiguous required candles are unresolved, not wins or losses, and are reported by group and baseline.

For each group, report Model A `n`, wins, losses, ties, unresolved, win rate, matched-baseline `n` and win rate, and the paired win-rate difference (Model A minus baseline). The primary estimand is the mean within-pair binary outcome difference. Report all three groups separately and report all paired contrasts among the three groups.

Do not reconstruct or rewrite prior snapshots. Report eligible and excluded Model A groups by symbol/profile and by exclusion reason. ## Confidence intervals and multiplicity

Use 10,000 paired moving-block bootstrap replicates, resampling contiguous 10-calendar-day UTC blocks with replacement across the holdout while preserving all symbol/profile strata and Model A/control pairs within each sampled block. Ten days is the prespecified block length and is never shortened. Report pointwise 95% bootstrap confidence intervals for each group win rate, matched baseline win rate, paired difference, and pairwise group differences, plus the number of distinct holdout blocks represented. For each confirmatory test, calculate a two-sided centered block-bootstrap p-value by centering the replicate statistic distribution at zero and using the plus-one tail proportion. If fewer than ten usable holdout blocks exist, report the relevant interval and test as unavailable.

The confirmatory family contains exactly six two-sided tests at alpha 0.05: (1) attributed versus its matched baseline, (2) unattributed versus its matched baseline, (3) ambiguous versus its matched baseline, and (4–6) each pairwise contrast among the three Model A groups. Apply Holm step-down adjustment across all six tests. No other symbol-, timeframe-, profile-, subgroup-, or exploratory comparison is confirmatory; show those descriptively and label them exploratory.

Do not make an inferential superiority claim for a group unless it has at least 100 resolved Model A observations, at least 100 resolved matched controls, representation in at least ten usable holdout blocks, a two-sided paired 95% bootstrap interval for the difference excluding zero, and Holm-adjusted p < 0.05. Otherwise state plainly that the group is too small or imprecise to conclude. Sparse samples, unresolved outcomes, insufficient blocks, or missing data are not evidence of no edge.
